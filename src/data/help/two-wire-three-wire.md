# Two-wire and three-wire control
**Two-wire control:** a maintained switch (a selector, a float, a thermostat) runs the coil directly. If power drops and comes back, or an [[overload-relay]] resets, the motor **restarts by itself** because the switch is still closed.

**Three-wire control:** momentary Start and Stop buttons with a [[seal-in]]. After a power loss the seal-in has dropped, so the motor stays off until someone presses Start.

Pick two-wire when automatic restart is the point (a lift station pump on a float) and three-wire when an unexpected restart could hurt someone.

## Safety
With two-wire control, put the selector at OFF before resetting an overload. See [[stop-before-reset]].
