# Selector switch (SA)
A selector switch is a maintained knob (it stays where you put it) with two or three positions. Each position closes certain contacts. In this game, `POS(SA2,auto)` is a contact of SA2 that's closed only at Auto.

Our press: **SA2** Manual/Stop/Auto, **SA1** Local/Remote, **SA4 to SA6** mixer, feed and dosing in Manual, **SA11 and SA12** drive 1 and 2 OFF/ON.

## Real world
Drawings list a selector's contact blocks in a **truth table**: which contacts close in which position. SA11 and SA12 sit after wire 19 on our press, so they matter in Auto too, despite what the manual says.
