# Troubleshooting with a meter
Work along the rung that isn't doing its job, from the live side toward the load, with the circuit trying to run:

- Voltage on **both** sides of a closed contact: it's good; move on.
- Voltage on one side and **none** on the other of a contact that should be closed: that contact, or the wire right after it, is open.
- Voltage all the way to the coil and it still doesn't pull in: suspect the coil.

## Try it
Turn on **Meter** and tap wires in the drawing. The reading is from that wire to N.

## Safety
Voltage testing in a live panel is done by **qualified people** with a CAT III rated meter and the PPE NFPA 70E calls for. See [[live-work]].

## Real world
Check your meter on a known live source before and after testing for absence of voltage (live-dead-live).
