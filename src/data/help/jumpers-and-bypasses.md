# Jumpers and bypasses
A jumper is a wire across terminals that makes a contact always conduct. Technicians use them briefly to test or to keep something running, but a jumper left in **defeats whatever that contact was protecting**.

## Safety
- Never jumper a safety device (E-stop, guard switch, high-level cutoff) to keep running.
- Log every temporary jumper, tag it, and remove it before the machine goes back in service.

## Real world
Our press photos show yellow jumpers at the end of the terminal strip, where floats 31/32 and 36/37 should land (unconfirmed). If they are jumpers, the sludge and dosing pumps can run dry.
