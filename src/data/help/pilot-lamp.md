# Pilot lamp (H)
A pilot lamp shows the operator what's happening: running, faulted, power on. On our press they're tagged **H**: H5 and H16 are the screw run lamps (wired across the KM11 and KM12 coils), H30 to H34 are fault lamps on sheet 3, and H1 is Power.

## Real world
- A lamp wired across a contactor coil says "the coil has voltage", not "the motor is turning". A welded contactor or a faulted drive can make the lamp lie. See [[contactor]].
- Many panels have a **lamp test** button. Use it before trusting a dark lamp.
