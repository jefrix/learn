# Pushbutton (SB)
A momentary pushbutton closes its NO contact (or opens its NC contact) only while it's held. Our panels tag pushbuttons **SB**: SB3 is the press's Auto start, SB4 its Auto stop.

Colors follow a convention: green or black for start, red for stop, a red mushroom on yellow for an [[e-stop]].

## Real world
Industrial pushbuttons are 22 mm operators with snap-on contact blocks, so one button can have several NO and NC contacts. A Stop button is wired through its **NC** block. See [[fail-safe]].
