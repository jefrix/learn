# Working on live panels
Opening an energized panel exposes you to shock and arc flash. US rules (NFPA 70E, OSHA) say equipment should be put in an **electrically safe work condition** (locked out and tested dead) unless live work is truly necessary, such as voltage testing while troubleshooting.

## Safety
- Only qualified people do live testing, with the right PPE and a rated meter.
- In this game you can probe anything with no risk. On the real press, you can't.

## Real world
Our press runs 230 VAC control and three-phase power to the drives. Even with the E-stop pressed, the drives and the Power lamp stay live. See [[e-stop]].
